# Privacy Policy – Shruti Tech Demo

**Last updated: November 7, 2026**

Shruti Tech Demo collects the name and email address submitted through its test lead form.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the submitted information only for:
- Testing our lead integration system.
- Retrieving submitted lead details through the Meta Graph API.
- Displaying lead information in our demonstration application.

## Data Sharing and Storage

Submitted information is received by our backend server and displayed in our demo application. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

## Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your submitted information by contacting the administrator of Shruti Tech Demo.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the administrator of Shruti Tech Demo.
